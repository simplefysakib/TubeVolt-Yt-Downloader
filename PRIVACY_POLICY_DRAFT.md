# TubeVolt privacy notice — draft

**Effective date:** 22 November 2026. **Publisher:** simplefy.

This draft describes the app's current implementation at a high level; it is not legal advice and must be checked against the final APK, Unity Ads settings, applicable law and store requirements before publication.

## Data and external services

TubeVolt has no developer-operated account or analytics backend in this source. The app includes Unity Ads and requests the Android advertising-ID permission. Unity Ads and its service providers may process advertising identifiers, device/diagnostic signals and ad interaction information under their current policies and configuration. Video metadata and media requests are sent to external services; those providers may receive IP address, requested URL and normal connection information. Review the current provider policies and complete required consent and store disclosure forms before distribution.

## On-device data and permissions

Download files, download history and preferences are stored locally on the device unless the user chooses to share content. Android storage access is used to save/manage downloaded media. Notification permission is used for download status alerts.

## Clipboard

When the app is foregrounded, it may inspect clipboard text to identify a supported video link. Clipboard contents are not intentionally transmitted by the app as a separate clipboard payload. Users should not copy sensitive information while using clipboard detection.

## Choices, retention and contact

Users can manage permissions in Android settings and disable notifications in app settings. Data stored locally can be removed by deleting files or clearing app data. The publisher should add a monitored privacy contact address to the relevant store listing before release; no contact address was supplied for this draft.

## Children's privacy

The publisher must confirm the intended age group, advertising configuration and applicable child-protection requirements before release. Do not distribute as child-directed without the required review and safeguards.
